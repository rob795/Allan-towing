# Brand Guide

## Logo Usage
- Use the full logo only where the rendered area is large enough to stay readable.
- Use the compact icon in tight spaces such as mobile header and favicon contexts.
- Preserve clear space around the logo and do not crop, stretch, or distort it.

## Minimum Size
- Full logo: use at least a readable header/footer size.
- Compact icon: use for small UI locations and browser icons.
- Do not use the full logo in small favicon-scale placements.

## Clear Space
- Keep visible space around the logo so text and marks do not touch adjacent elements.
- Avoid placing the logo against busy backgrounds unless the background is controlled.

## Approved Colors
- Primary: #111111
- Secondary: #FFC400
- Accent: #D32F2F
- Background: #FFFFFF
- Light section: #F6F6F6
- Text: #1A1A1A

## Typography
- Headings: Oswald
- Body: Inter
- Keep the heading style bold, uppercase, and compact.
- Keep body copy clear and readable for emergency-service use.

## Icons
- Use the compact A247 icon for favicon and compact brand placements.
- Keep icons consistent with the approved color palette.

## Hero Usage
- Use the hero image as a temporary promotional visual.
- Do not claim the hero photo is verified company fleet photography.
- Keep text readable with a controlled dark overlay.

## Photography Style
- Commercial, realistic, service-oriented, and clean.
- Prefer real trucks, dispatch, roadside, and operational scenes.
- Avoid stock-photo generic visuals.

## Image Style
- High clarity
- No visible white halos on logos
- No oversharpening
- No embedded promotional copy inside photos
- Self-hosted optimized formats preferred

## Spacing
- Keep strong separation between logo and surrounding UI.
- Do not crowd the header or footer branding.

## Things NOT to do
- Do not force the full logo into favicon slots.
- Do not place unverified imagery into factual schema claims.
- Do not stretch or recolor the logo outside the approved palette.
- Do not add decorative treatments that reduce clarity.
